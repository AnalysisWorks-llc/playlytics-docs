# Quality At-Bats (QAB%)

## Overview

Quality At-Bat percentage measures productive plate appearances beyond just batting average. A high QAB% indicates good plate discipline and situational hitting — even when a player isn't getting hits.

## What Counts as a Quality At-Bat

A plate appearance counts as a Quality At-Bat when the batter:

- **Gets a hit** — single, double, triple, or home run
- **Draws a walk** or gets **hit by pitch**
- **Hits a sacrifice fly or sacrifice bunt** that advances a runner
- **Records an RBI on a productive out**
- **Makes hard contact** — even if the at-bat results in an out
- **Advances a runner to scoring position**
- **Has a long at-bat** — typically 6 or more pitches, forcing the pitcher to work

## Logging Quality At-Bat Components

Most QAB criteria are detected automatically from your existing at-bat logging — hits, walks, HBP, sacrifices, RBI, and hard-hit balls are all already tracked.

Two additional buttons in the At-Bat Logger let you log the remaining criteria:

**🏃 Adv Runner** — tap when the at-bat advances a baserunner into scoring position, even if the batter is out.

**⏱ Long AB** — tap when the at-bat goes 6 or more pitches, indicating the batter worked a tough at-bat against the pitcher.

## Age-Appropriate Thresholds

QAB% goals vary by age group since plate discipline develops over time:

| Age Group | QAB% Goal |
|-----------|-----------|
| 8U - 10U | 40% |
| 11U - 12U | 45% |
| 13U - 14U | 50% |
| 15U - 18U | 55% |

Playlytics automatically applies the correct threshold based on your team's level setting.

## Where QAB% Appears

**Individual player hitting analytics** — a dedicated QAB% stat card shows the player's percentage, a progress bar with the age-appropriate goal marked, and a breakdown of which QAB types contributed (hits, walks, hard hit balls, etc).

**Team analytics leaderboard** — players are ranked by QAB% with a minimum of 5 plate appearances required to qualify for ranking.

**Player report cards** — QAB% appears in the hitting stats section and in the team comparison table, showing whether the player meets the team threshold.

**Team hitting detail chart** — shows the team's overall QAB% alongside the spray chart and contact quality breakdown.

**Training suggestions** — if a player's QAB% falls below the age-appropriate goal, Playlytics generates a training suggestion focused on plate discipline, two-strike approach, and situational hitting.

## Why QAB% Matters

Batting average only counts hits. A player who consistently hits the ball hard, draws walks, and battles through long at-bats is contributing to winning baseball even during a slump in batting average. QAB% captures that contribution and gives coaches a more complete picture of a hitter's approach at the plate.
